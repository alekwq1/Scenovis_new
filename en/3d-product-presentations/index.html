FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What data do you need for a 3D product presentation?

CAD models (e.g. STEP) or technical drawings work best. If you don’t have them, we can start from photos, dimensions and a description of how it works — and agree the right level of detail together.

Can the presentation be used after the fair?

Yes. The model and materials prepared for the fair can later support sales, a showroom, or be used as video and images on your website.

How much does an exhibition stand design cost?

It depends on the scope: floor space, the number of options and whether you need a 3D presentation or multimedia. I quote the design after a short call, before any work starts.
