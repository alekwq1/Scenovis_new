FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the stand design process look like?

We start with a brief: the fair, dates, floor space, goal and budget. We then prepare the concept and functional layout, a 3D model and a list of elements with their cost type. We go through the options, and only then do you decide what to order from contractors.

How do you price a project?

Every project is quoted individually — there are no fixed packages. The quote depends on the scope: for a stand, for example, on floor space and the number of options; for a 3D presentation, on the complexity of the product and the format. We give you a quote after a short call, before any work starts.

What drives the cost of a stand?

Usually: floor space and build type (modular or custom), materials and finish, graphics, lighting, multimedia, furniture, transport, build-up and dismantling, plus organiser fees (e.g. utility connections). For your project we break this down element by element.

What is better to rent and what to buy?

Mostly it depends on how often you exhibit. Elements used repeatedly — such as a modular structure, a kiosk or universal graphics — are often worth buying. AV equipment dates quickly, so renting it is usually better. In the project we compare both options for your situation.

Can the stand be reused at future fairs?

Yes, if we plan for it from the start: modules, replaceable graphics, transport and storage. We ask about it in the brief, because it affects the buy / rent decision.

What is needed to start?

Enough to start: the fair and its dates, floor space and stand type (island, corner, inline), what you want to show and a rough budget. Also helpful: logo or brand guide, product photos or models (e.g. STEP) and the organiser’s technical rules.

Can you handle the whole delivery?

Yes. Stand build, multimedia, transport, installation, stand staff and on-site coordination are organised within the scope we agree — we assemble the team and contractors for the project. You can also order just the design with a cost breakdown and build it with your own contractor.

How early should we start?

Ideally start 2–3 months before the fair, and earlier for a custom-built or large stand. Organisers often require design approval in advance, and contractors need time for quotes and production.

With a smaller scope and materials ready (stand dimensions, logo, product data) we can work faster. We agree a specific schedule after the brief.
